# Tibometer v1.0.2

- Restore automatic connection after Codex app updates.
- Support selecting the updated Codex app in data source settings.

## Download

`Tibometer-1.0.2-macOS-arm64.zip` requires Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.

Quit Tibometer, unzip the archive, and replace `Tibometer.app` in Applications. Your settings are preserved.

This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. macOS may block opening it; you can also [build from source](https://github.com/nangcr/Tibometer#install).

To verify the download, place `SHA256SUMS` beside the archive and run:

```bash
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS
```
